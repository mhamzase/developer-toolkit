Why developers pick Zase

Most online tools send what you paste to a server. Zase was built to never do that, on this site and in the extension.

Private by design

Formatting, decoding, hashing and generating all happen locally on your device. Tokens and payloads never travel anywhere.

Works fully offline

The Chrome extension runs with no connection at all. On the web, every tool works right in the tab, with nothing to install.

Instant, no waiting

No uploads, no queues, no server round trips. Paste, click, done: results appear as fast as your device can compute them.

Free, no account

Every tool is free, with no sign up, no trial timer and no locked features. Open a page and start working.

Chrome extension

Every tool, one shortcut away

Developer Toolkit by Zase packs the same tools into a free Chrome extension: fully offline, in a standalone window or docked in the side panel, with favorites, recents and a right-click menu that sends selected text straight into a tool.
